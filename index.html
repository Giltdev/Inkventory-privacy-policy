Inkventory

Fountain Pen & Ink Collection Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 26, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 26, 2026

Inkventory is designed to work fully offline. Your pen and ink collection data is stored on your device first. Cloud sync is optional and only activates if you choose to create an account.

1. Who We Are

Inkventory is an independent mobile application for Android and iOS. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at 0gpdev0@gmail.com.

2. What Data We Collect

Collection data stored locally

Pen names, brands, models, nib sizes, photos, notes; ink names, brands, color values, property tags (shading, sheen, shimmer), level indicators, notes; ink change timestamps and history; rotation reminder settings.

All of this is stored on your device using AsyncStorage. It never leaves your device unless you explicitly sign in to enable cloud sync.

Account & cloud sync data optional · cloud

If you create an account (email, Google Sign-In, or Apple Sign-In), we store your email address and a unique user ID in our Supabase database. Your collection data is then synced to the cloud so you can restore it on a new device. Cloud data is protected by Row Level Security — only you can access your own records.

Subscription & purchase data third-party

If you purchase Inkventory Premium, payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives your anonymised app user ID and purchase receipt. We do not receive or store your payment card details. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Photos stored locally

You may optionally attach photos to pens and ink bottles using your device camera or photo library. Photos are stored on your device. If cloud sync is enabled, photos are uploaded to Supabase Storage and associated with your account. We request camera and photo library permissions only when you initiate a photo action — we do not access your camera or photos passively.

Push notifications on-device only

Inkventory uses local scheduled notifications for pen rotation reminders. No notification tokens are transmitted to any server. Notifications are scheduled entirely on-device and can be cancelled at any time from the app or your device settings.

3. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect usage analytics or behavioural tracking data.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or sell data to advertisers.
  • We do not build profiles for targeted advertising.
  • We do not share your data with third parties except as described in Section 4.
  • We do not access your contacts, location, microphone, or any sensor not listed above.

4. Third-Party Services

The app integrates with the following services, each with their own privacy policy:

  • Supabase — database and authentication provider (optional, only when signed in). Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — subscription management (only when purchasing Premium). Privacy Policy
  • Google — payment processing and sign-in. Google: Privacy Policy

5. Data Storage & Security

Local data is stored using the platform's standard secure storage (AsyncStorage on Android and iOS). Cloud data is stored in Supabase's infrastructure (EU region) with Row Level Security enabled, meaning database queries are enforced at the row level so no user can access another user's data.

We do not guarantee absolute security of any data transmitted over the internet. Use of the cloud sync feature is at your own discretion.

6. Data Retention

Local data — remains on your device until you uninstall the app or delete items manually.

Cloud data — retained for the duration of your account. Deleting your account removes all associated records from our database within 30 days.

CSV export — is always available and always free. You can export your full collection at any time from Settings.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — export a full copy of your data at any time via the CSV export in Settings.
  • Deletion — delete your account and all associated cloud data by contacting us at 0gpdev0@gmail.com.
  • Portability — use the CSV export to take your data to any other service or spreadsheet.
  • Opt out of cloud sync — simply do not create an account. The app is fully functional offline.

If you are in the EU/EEA, you also have rights under the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection). Contact us to exercise these rights.

8. Children's Privacy

Inkventory is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be announced inside the app. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the current version. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
