Inkventory

Fountain Pen & Ink Collection Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 26, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 30, 2026

Inkventory is designed to work fully offline. Your pen and ink collection data is stored on your device first. Cloud sync and analytics are optional or limited in scope and are described in full below.

1. Who We Are

Inkventory is an independent mobile application for Android developed by an individual developer. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at 0gpdev0@gmail.com.

2. What Data We Collect

Collection data stored locally

Pen names, brands, models, nib sizes, photos, and notes; ink names, brands, color values, property tags (shading, sheen, shimmer), level indicators, and notes; ink change timestamps and history; rotation reminder settings.

All of this is stored on your device using AsyncStorage (unencrypted local storage). It never leaves your device unless you explicitly sign in and enable cloud sync. Authentication session tokens are stored separately in the device's secure Keystore via expo-secure-store.

Account & cloud sync data optional · cloud

If you create an account using your email address (sign-in is via a one-time code sent to your email), we store your email address and a unique user ID in our Supabase database hosted in the EU (eu-west-1). Your collection data is then synced to the cloud so you can restore it on a new device. Cloud data is protected by Row Level Security: only your account can read or write your records.

Photos locally first

You may optionally attach photos to pens and ink bottles. Photos are stored on your device. If cloud sync is enabled, photos are uploaded to Supabase Storage and associated with your account. We request camera and photo library permissions only when you initiate a photo action. We do not access your camera or photos passively.

Usage analytics third-party · anonymous

Inkventory uses PostHog (hosted in the EU) to collect anonymous usage events that help us understand how the app is used and improve it. These events include actions such as: adding or deleting a pen or ink, completing an ink change, viewing the paywall, purchasing Premium, enabling cloud sync, and exporting data.

These events do not include the content of your collection (pen names, ink names, colors, notes, or photos). No personally identifiable information is attached to these events. PostHog receives an anonymous device identifier. See the PostHog Privacy Policy.

Subscription & purchase data third-party

If you purchase Inkventory Premium, payments are processed by Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives your anonymous app user ID and purchase receipt. We do not receive or store your payment card details. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Push notifications on-device only

Inkventory uses local scheduled notifications for pen rotation reminders. Notifications are scheduled entirely on-device and no notification tokens are transmitted to any server. You can cancel reminders at any time from the app or your device settings.

3. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect the content of your collection (pen names, ink names, colors, notes, or photos) for analytics purposes.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or sell data to advertisers.
  • We do not build profiles for targeted advertising.
  • We do not share your personal data with third parties except as described in Section 4.
  • We do not access your contacts, location, microphone, or any sensor not listed in Section 2.
  • We do not use Google Sign-In, Facebook Login, or any social authentication. Sign-in is by email only (one-time code).

4. Third-Party Services

The app integrates with the following services, each governed by their own privacy policy:

  • Supabase (EU region) - database, authentication, and file storage provider. Only active when you are signed in with cloud sync enabled. Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat - subscription and purchase management. Only active when purchasing or restoring Premium. Privacy Policy
  • PostHog (EU region) - anonymous product analytics. Active in all builds. Only receives anonymous event names and a device identifier, never collection content. Privacy Policy
  • Google Play - payment processing for in-app purchases. Privacy Policy

5. Data Storage and Security

Local collection data (pens, inks, history) is stored using AsyncStorage, which is standard unencrypted local storage on Android. Authentication session tokens are stored in the device's secure Keystore via expo-secure-store.

Cloud data is stored in Supabase's infrastructure in the EU (eu-west-1 region) with Row Level Security enabled. This means every database query is enforced at the row level so no user can access another user's data. Data in transit is encrypted using TLS.

We apply reasonable technical measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. Use of the cloud sync feature is at your own discretion.

6. Data Retention

Local data remains on your device until you uninstall the app or delete items manually within the app.

Cloud data is retained for as long as your account is active. Deleting your account removes all associated records from our database within 30 days.

Analytics data retained by PostHog is subject to PostHog's own retention policy.

CSV export is always available and always free. You can export your full collection at any time from Settings.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access - export a full copy of your collection data at any time via the CSV export in Settings.
  • Deletion - delete your account and all associated cloud data by emailing 0gpdev0@gmail.com with your registered email address. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.
  • Portability - use the CSV export to take your data to any other service or spreadsheet.
  • Opt out of cloud sync - do not create an account. The app is fully functional offline with no cloud sync.
  • Opt out of analytics - analytics are anonymous and do not contain personal data. If you wish to request that your anonymous analytics data be deleted, contact us and we will submit a deletion request to PostHog on your behalf.

If you are in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have rights under the GDPR including the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, and objection. Contact us at 0gpdev0@gmail.com to exercise these rights.

8. Children's Privacy

Inkventory is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the current version. For material changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify users within the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
